Use this guide for hosted 8Labs services. Start with the task you need.

## Find the right panel

- Use **[Client Panel](https://my.8labs.id)** for orders, invoices, and payments.
- Use **[Cloud Panel](https://cloud.8labs.id)** to manage your server and who can access it.

Open your purchased service in Client Panel to find its management link. Do not assume both panels use the same password.

## Get connected

1. [Order and find your server](/docs/getting-started/intro/).
2. [Choose SSH, RDP, or the browser console](/docs/getting-started/accessing-virtual-labs/).
3. [Understand the server workspace](/docs/servers/overview/).

## Manage a running server

- [Power and busy states](/docs/servers/power/) — stop safely before considering force controls.
- [Network addresses](/docs/servers/network/) and [firewall rules](/docs/servers/firewall/) — understand the path before changing it.
- [Backups](/docs/servers/backups/) and [snapshots](/docs/servers/snapshots/) — choose the right recovery point.
- [Telemetry and activity](/docs/servers/telemetry/) — distinguish observations from missing data.
- [Reinstall and deletion](/docs/servers/settings/) — review irreversible impact first.

## Work with other people

[Teams and permissions](/docs/teams/overview/) explains organizations, projects, and single-server access.
Keep your [account secure](/docs/account/security/) and use separate [API keys](/docs/account/api-keys/) for automation.
Manage [saved SSH public keys](/docs/account/security/#ssh-keys), [account-wide backups](/docs/servers/backups/#find-backups-across-your-account), and [reusable firewall templates](/docs/servers/firewall/#manage-account-firewall-templates) without confusing them with changes inside an existing guest.
Connect optional [Telegram notifications](/docs/account/notifications/) only when the hosted service offers them.

## Separate guest setup from product controls

The [Docker](/docs/getting-started/install-docker-ipv6/), [IPv6 connectivity](/docs/getting-started/ipv6-github-dockerhub/), [guest agent](/docs/getting-started/install-qemu-guest-agent/), [aaPanel](/docs/getting-started/panel/aapanel-installation/), and [Hermes](/docs/getting-started/hermes-agent-ipv6/) tutorials run inside your guest.
These English tutorials do not establish a managed-software entitlement, image availability, or a guaranteed third-party network route.

## Get help

Check [troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/overview/), [billing](/docs/billing-plans/overview/), or [contact support](/docs/help/contact/).
An absent button can mean a missing permission or an unavailable hosted feature.

General [guides](/guides/) and the [blog](/blog/) retain their existing English articles. They are tutorials, not hosted service guarantees.
